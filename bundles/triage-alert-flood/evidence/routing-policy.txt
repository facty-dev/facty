SECURITY ALERT ROUTING POLICY — SOC tier-1, current revision

When an alert flood arrives, tier-1 routes it by 06:00 using three
dispositions:

P1 — targeted attack. Route to incident response; hand off to
     attribution (identify the operator). Trigger: a coordinated set of
     attack-classified alerts from a small number of sources in a
     short window, confirmed against threat intelligence.

P2 — detector false-positive flood. Route to detection engineering
     for signature tuning. Trigger: the flood is mostly benign
     activity misflagged by the SIEM.

P3 — background scanning noise. Route to the tier-1 suppression queue.
     Trigger: the flood is internet background radiation — scans
     from many unrelated sources, no attack-classified activity.

The disposition is a routing decision, not an accusation: the pack
computes it from the classified evidence; the human owns the call.
