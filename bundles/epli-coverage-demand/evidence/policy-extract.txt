MERIDIAN SPECIALTY INSURANCE — EMPLOYMENT PRACTICES LIABILITY
POLICY MPL-2026-4471 (claims-made) — EXTRACT

Insuring clause (Section I.A):
  "This policy covers Loss arising from any Employment Practice Wrongful Act,
   including termination, discrimination, harassment and retaliation claims
   brought by an employee or applicant."

Claims-made and reported (Section VI):
  "The Insured must give written notice of any Claim within NINETY (90) DAYS
   after the Claim is first made."

Internal complaint condition (Section VII):
  "The Insured shall use its internal complaint procedure in good faith before
   any Claim is made."

Exclusions (Section IV) — full list:
  IV.1 Fraud or dishonesty (final adjudication required)
  IV.2 Bodily injury / property damage
  IV.3 ERISA / benefits
  IV.4 Pending prior litigation (as of 2026-01-01)
  No other exclusions are endorsed onto this policy.
