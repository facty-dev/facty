MOOR REPORT — the night's find, approx. 04:30

Body of John Straker found on the moor path. Skull fractured — a single
blow. Cravat torn. Penknife and lantern beside him. No sign of a struggle
with a stranger; one set of boot-prints near the body, stable boots.

Silver Blaze's hoof-prints leave the stable, cross the moor path (gait:
walking, not led), and continue toward Mapleton.
