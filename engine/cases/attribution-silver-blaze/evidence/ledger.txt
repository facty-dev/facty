LEDGER — Straker's second establishment (found in his rooms)

Rent paid under another name. Entries:

- "S.B." → J.S., weekly, "for keeping the horse sound" — payments to
  keep Silver Blaze from racing until after the Cup.
- Final entry, night of the disappearance: "J.S. to meet N.H. on the
  moor, 3 a.m., to collect the last payment. N.H. to handle the horse."
