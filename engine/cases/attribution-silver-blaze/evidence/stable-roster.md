# King's Pyland — stable roster

- **Colonel Ross** — owner. Silver Blaze (champion, white forefoot) stabled here.
- **John Straker** — trainer, twenty years in Colonel Ross's service.
- **Ned Hunter** — stable lad; knew Straker from the city years back.
- **Carlo** — kennel spaniel. Barks at strangers; knows the stable staff.
- **Silas Brown** — rival trainer, Mapleton stables (across the moor).
